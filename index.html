DIGITAL HEALTH PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

AI-Driven Discharge Planning: Reducing "ALC" Bed Blocking

A predictive modeling project aimed at solving hospital "gridlock." Due to PHIPA privacy constraints, I designed a Monte Carlo Simulation based on 2024 CIHI aggregate data to generate a synthetic patient population. I then trained a Random Forest model to identify patients at risk of Alternate Level of Care (ALC) status upon admission.

Key Findings

  • Root Cause Identified: The model revealed that "Waiting for Long-Term Care" and "Dementia Diagnosis" were 3x more predictive of delays than patient age alone.
  • Operational Impact: Proposed a "Digital Command Center" workflow where high-risk patients are flagged for Social Work intervention within 24 hours of admission.
